# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the
SynthAudit-Bench community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation. We adopt the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## Scientific-conduct clause

SynthAudit-Bench audits **datasets, not people**. Findings describe structural
properties of released files; they are never framed as judgments of the
competence or intent of dataset authors. Reports that name a dataset follow the
responsible-disclosure procedure in `SECURITY.md`. Datasets that describe people
contain synthetic records only; contributors must not attempt re-identification.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the maintainers at erwinrandy3@gmail.com. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
